OFFLINE DICTIONARY DATA NOTICE

This application contains a transformed English-Russian lexical dataset.

1. English frequency ranking
Source: FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave
Corpus basis: OpenSubtitles 2018
Project: https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
Content license: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)

2. English-Russian translation pairs
Source: MUSE bilingual dictionaries, Facebook Research / Meta Research
Project: https://github.com/facebookresearch/MUSE
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0)

Transformation performed for this application:
- retained individual English tokens with Cyrillic Russian translations;
- ranked eligible English entries using the frequency list;
- retained the first 15,000 unique eligible English entries;
- ranked Russian variants by Russian subtitle frequency where available;
- retained up to 10 Russian variants per English entry;
- serialized the result as compact JSON.

The bundled dataset is intended for personal, noncommercial educational use.
Keep this notice with redistributed copies.
